01 · Overview

Project Overview

Chinese New Year Cookie Feast is an interactive Three.js food animation created from procedurally modeled OpenSCAD geometry. The project explores how different food materials can be communicated through both visual appearance and physical motion.

Almond Pastry

Brittle Material

The almond pastry uses baked surface textures, roughness variation, geometry switching, exposed filling, and crumb particles to communicate a dry and brittle structure.

Dry Rough Brittle Crumbly
Crystal Osmanthus Cake

Soft Material

The crystal cake combines transparency, transmission, clearcoat, squash and stretch, and procedural wobbling to communicate a soft jelly-like material.

Soft Elastic Translucent Jelly-like

06 · Materials

Texture Maps

The almond pastry uses separate base-color and roughness maps. Because the STL geometry does not provide useful UV coordinates, these textures are applied through custom triplanar shader projection.
